# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the person you're replying to is acting in good
faith and knows things you don't.

## What's expected

- Keep criticism about the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change without that being a judgement
  on you. This is a small project with opinions about scope.
- Remember that most contributors are doing this in spare time, in a second
  language, across time zones.

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption of
discussions, or publishing someone's private information. This applies in
issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/lammatrac). Reports stay confidential. The
maintainer may edit or delete comments, close threads, or block accounts, and
will say when they have.

Since this is a solo project, the maintainer is also the person you'd report the
maintainer to — which is an obvious limitation rather than a pretence otherwise.
If that's not workable for you, GitHub's own
[abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) is independent of
this project.

## Attribution

The spirit of this document follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), shortened to what
a project this size can actually honour.
