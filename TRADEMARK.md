# Trademark policy

The WayContext **source code** is licensed under Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`).
The WayContext **name and logo** are not. They are trademarks of the project
maintainers, and the Apache-2.0 license grants no trademark rights
(see section 6 of the license).

This is deliberate. The permissive license exists so anyone can use, fork,
embed, and build on the code, commercially or otherwise. The trademark exists
so that "WayContext" continues to mean one thing to the people who rely on it.

## You do not need permission to

- Use, modify, fork, redistribute, or sell products built on the code, under
  the terms of Apache-2.0.
- State factually that your product "uses WayContext", "is built on
  WayContext", or "is compatible with WayContext".
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons, and reviews.
- Keep the name in an unmodified redistribution of the project.

## You do need permission to

- Name your product, service, company, or domain "WayContext" or something
  confusingly similar.
- Offer a hosted or commercial service under the WayContext name.
- Use the logo as your own product's mark, or modify it.
- Suggest that your fork is the official project, or that we endorse,
  sponsor, or support your product.

If you distribute a **modified** version, please give it your own name and
describe it as "based on WayContext" rather than as WayContext itself. This
keeps bug reports and security advisories pointed at the right project.

Questions, or want permission? Open an issue.
